# Hi, I'm Jorge (Remiotore)

**Offensive security. Security-minded, systems-driven.**

I am a Certified Penetration Testing Specialist (CPTS - Hack The Box).

I have a background of 4+5 years (studying + working) as a FullStack Developer (web2 and web3), DevOps, SysAdmin, and SOC analyst.

Over the last year I focused on a security-related path as a pentester, solving and hacking machines and then passing the CPTS certification — a 10-day exam where you have to pwn 7 servers through different vulnerabilities (Active Directory, misconfigurations, web attacks, and more).

## Services

- **Leaks & OSINT** — credential exposure checks, personal-data exposure checks, and typosquatting monitoring.
- **Network & host testing** — review of approved IPs, domains, hosts, and network ranges.
- **Web applications & API security** — focused testing of web apps and REST, GraphQL, gRPC, or SOAP APIs.
- **Active Directory assessment** — authorized review of AD configuration, identity security, and common attack paths.
- **Mobile application testing** — iOS and Android assessment planning (work in progress).
- **Repository review** — security review of individual repositories or agreed organization scope.
